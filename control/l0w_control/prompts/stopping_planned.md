## When to stop

A wakeup's budget is planned, not use-it-or-lose-it: what you don't spend stays in the period's
allowance (except in a sweep wakeup, where it's lost at the reset). When there's nothing worthwhile
left, make `IDLE: <reason>` the last line of your final message. Don't invent busywork to spend
budget. If you stop with a lot of budget left and no `IDLE:`, control asks you to carry on or say why
you're done.
